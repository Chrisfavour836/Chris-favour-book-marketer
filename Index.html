Simple Marketing Glossary

Plain-language meanings for terms you may see in my reports.

Impressions
The number of times your ad or post was shown.
CTR
Click-through rate: the share of people who clicked after seeing it.
CPC
Cost per click: what you pay each time someone clicks your ad.
Conversion
When a visitor does what you hoped, such as buying your book.
ACOS
Advertising cost of sales: ad spend compared with the sales the ads brought in.
Keywords
Words and phrases readers type when searching for books.
Open rate
The share of people who opened your email.
Click rate
The share of people who clicked a link inside your email.
Unsubscribe rate
The share of people who left your email list after an email.
Organic vs paid
Organic reach is free and earned. Paid reach comes from advertising.
Audience
The group of people most likely to enjoy your book.
Positioning
How your book is presented so readers see what makes it different.
